FAQs

What does the app do?

  • Allows local artists to promote themselves
  • Promotes local events and venues
  • Allows customers to buy event tickets directly from the venues

How does the app find local events?

Venues and promoters can register their events or they can upload advertisements and flyers.

Does it cost money to advertise on the app?

Nope! Anyone can promote their content or their events free of charge.
