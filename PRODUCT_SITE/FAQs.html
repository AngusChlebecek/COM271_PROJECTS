How It Works FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What does LocalScene do?

  • Helps local artists to promote themselves
  • Promotes local events and venues
  • Allows customers to avoid scalpers and buy event tickets directly from local venues

How does LocalScene find local events?

Venues and promoters can register their events or they can upload advertisements/flyers.

Does it cost money to advertise on LocalScene?

Nope! Anyone can promote their content or their events free of charge.
