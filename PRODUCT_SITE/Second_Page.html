How Does It Work?

Creating An Event

To create an event, you input a name, a location, and a time. You can also upload a thumbnail image. Then you press "upload"

Creating An Artist Profile

To create an artist profile, you input your name, your base location, and pictures. You can then provide links to your music platforms, and links to any events you have uploaded

What can you use it for?

Promoting your venue
Promoting your art
Advertising local events
